Strategies tuned to your program goals

Stack value through 8 load shaping strategies.

Every Optiwatt program is built around the strategies that matter most to you — from event-driven dispatch to continuous shaping, with measurable results from day one.

DR / Emergency Events

Reduce load on an ad hoc basis to avoid high costs during temporary periods of grid stress, like a hot summer afternoon. Avoid snapback demand with secondary peak mitigation.

Daily Peak Avoidance

Shift device load out of the daily peak hours, and avoid snapback demand with secondary peak mitigation.

TOU Optimization

Lower customer bills by shifting energy usage out of the most expensive hours, and avoid snapback demand with secondary peak mitigation.

Wholesale Price Optimization

Lower your costs by shifting energy usage to low-cost wholesale hours (for example, CAISO hourly rates).

Clean Power Hours

Soak up renewable power when generation is high — encourage EV charging and thermostat pre-cool and pre-heat during clean-energy windows.

Peak Smoothing

Continuous load shaping that flattens daily peaks to even out the load curve every day.

Demand Limit

Capacity-managed dispatch windows that hold power below a defined ceiling — useful for distribution-system relief at the feeder and substation level when targeting groups of devices based on their location.

GHG Optimization

Shift energy use to low-carbon hours, based on real-time grid carbon intensity.
