Partnership announcement

Rivian and Optiwatt collaborate to deliver customer-first managed charging built for scale.

A direct, authorized OEM integration provides a seamless charging experience, unlocks utility program value for EV owners, and establishes a secure blueprint for grid load management.

For immediate release · Walnut Creek, CA

Walnut Creek, CA. Optiwatt, a leading provider of consumer-facing managed charging and home energy optimization software, today announced an agreement with Rivian to deliver direct, authorized electric vehicle (EV) managed charging infrastructure to Rivian drivers across North America. This foundational integration marks a major milestone in Optiwatt's ongoing strategic expansion to scale direct, secure original equipment manufacturer (OEM) pipelines across the automotive industry.

By establishing an authenticated, API-based architecture directly with Rivian's software-defined vehicles, this infrastructure ensures the highest standards of hardware compliance, data privacy, and end-user system reliability. It provides a stable and highly scalable flexible energy resource for energy providers nationwide, while helping EV drivers who opt in to utility programs get more value out of their vehicle through lower home charging costs.

A new blueprint for driver and grid integration

As home charging accounts for the vast majority of EV energy demand, utilities and automotive OEMs have an opportunity to support a more resilient energy grid by helping coordinate load scaling effectively. The Optiwatt and Rivian collaboration prioritizes the driver ecosystem, built on three foundational pillars:

01

Enhanced user experience

Smart charging is re-engineered into a premium, completely seamless ecosystem. Rivian drivers can securely link their vehicles via official, authenticated APIs in seconds, yielding a sophisticated energy companion that runs quietly and reliably in the background. This lowers sign-up complexity and lets customers start maximizing the value of their Rivian vehicles.

02

Value through program participation

Moving far beyond basic scheduling, the platform dynamically manages charge windows around localized grid requirements, market pricing signals, and available capacity. This captures maximal structural value for the owner, pairing effortless utility rewards and incentive enrollments with automated grid stabilization.

03

Integrating with the Rivian ecosystem

Building grid capacity requires program scale. By positioning managed charging education directly within Rivian's trusted channel and community interfaces, drivers are guided seamlessly into programs through a trusted relationship, driving unprecedented scale while supporting utility partners.

The engineering, product, and grid-services teams at Optiwatt and Rivian are actively collaborating on the architecture and end-to-end integration mapping. The direct integration is being designed from the ground up to support mass enrollment across major utility service territories throughout North America, turning stationary adventure vehicles into active grid assets without interrupting the daily routines of owners.

About Optiwatt

Optiwatt is a premier home energy optimization platform specializing in electric vehicle managed charging and localized grid telematics. Built on zero-friction, direct OEM-authorized connections, Optiwatt matches driver behavior with real-time grid economics to automate carbon reductions and utility cost savings, working with leading automakers and utility operators nationwide to build scalable, resilient Virtual Power Plants (VPPs). About Rivian
