Utility programs

We made friends with your utility so you don't have to.

Optiwatt partners with utilities across North America to bring energy programs right to your phone. Some pay cash or bill credits for shifting charging when the grid's crowded. Others are clean-grid initiatives without a payout. All of them, you can join with a tap.

Available across 36+ utility programs and growing. Rewards (when offered) come from your utility. Optiwatt just makes them easy to find, join, and actually collect.

  • Cash, credits, or just good karma.

    Some programs have paid drivers up to $650 in cash or bill credits for participating. Others let you help the grid stay clean without a payout. Your utility decides. We just show you what's on the table.

  • Auto-apply to available programs.

    Apply to programs one by one, or flip on Support My Local Grid and we'll automatically apply you to every program you qualify for. No portals, no fine-print PDFs.

  • You're always in control.

    Need to charge urgently during a demand response event? Start a charge at any time and we'll simply opt you out of the event. No penalties, no hassle.

So... what's the catch?

Wait, it's actually free?

No subscription. No upsell. The app is free because utilities pay us to shift charging off-peak.

Optiwatt isn't a freemium app. There's no trial that expires, no paywall sneaking in on page four, and no "premium tier" you're nudged toward.

Utility

Has a problem at 6 PM. When everyone gets home and plugs in at once, the grid strains and utilities have to fire up expensive backup plants. They'd really rather you charged at 2 AM.

Optiwatt

Solves it for them. We move charging to off-peak hours automatically. The utility pays us a small fee per car we shift. That's how we make money.

You

Get a smaller bill. Off-peak rates are cheaper, so you spend less on charging, and the app stays free. No trial, no paywall, no upsell. That's actually the whole pitch.
